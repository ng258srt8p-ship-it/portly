### Backend — `server/routes/cruises.ts`

Add `GET /api/history` endpoint:

1. **Query** `v_price_trends` grouped by `cruise_line` + `sailing_id`
2. **Return** aggregate per cruise line:
   - Line name, ship names
   - Price history (date → price data points)
   - Min/max/current prices
   - "Prices count" badge
3. **Schema:**
